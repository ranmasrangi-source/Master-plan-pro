## 1. Institutional Overview
**0 TO 8848** is an international adventure achievement recognition platform founded in Nepal for adventurers worldwide. It combines three connected but independently governed pillars:
1. **Pillar A — Global Achievement Recognition:** Multi-pillar Evidence Confidence Framework (`ECF`), SHA-256 chain-of-custody, independent human verification, separated appeals, free standard 3D metallic badges, printable QR certificates, and a zero-PII public verification portal.
2. **Pillar B — Global Adventure Marketplace:** License-verified guides, guide companies, tour operators, and safety-governed expedition listings.
3. **Pillar C — Global Adventure Network:** Community expedition logs, safety advisories, Leave No Trace (`NF-05`) environmental stewardship registry, and a separate historical Legends & Memorial archive.

## 2. Constitutional Trust Principles
> **ACHIEVEMENTS MUST BE EARNED, NEVER BOUGHT.**

- Standard digital badges, standard achievement certificates, public verification, and appeals are **100% Free Forever**.
- Sponsors, advertisers, and subscriptions are technically airgapped (`ReviewerCaseViewDTO` compile-time firewall) from verification and leaderboard calculations.
- Payment collection (`PAYMENTS_ENABLED = false`) and Blockchain minting (`BLOCKCHAIN_ENABLED = false`) are hard-disabled at launch until legal, regulatory, and security audits are formally authorized.
