## 1. Binding Amendment A2 — Macro-Domain Map (`MD-01` to `MD-06`)
In V1.1 Draft, `CAT-23` (Adaptive Adventure) and `CAT-24` (Environmental Stewardship) were listed under pseudo-domains ("Cross-Domain Inclusive" and "Civic / Non-Rank"), which violated a strict partition where every category maps to **exactly one** of the 6 official Macro-Domains (`MD-01` through `MD-06`).

**Amendment A2 Resolution:**
We define **6 Mutually Exclusive, Collectively Exhaustive Macro-Domains (`MD-01` to `MD-06`)**. Every single category from `CAT-01` through `CAT-24` maps to **one and only one** Macro-Domain ID (`MD-01`..`MD-06`), with `4 Categories × 6 Macro-Domains = 24 Categories` (or exact non-overlapping set partition):

### Macro-Domain Partition Verification Table (`MD-01` to `MD-06`)

| Macro-Domain ID | Macro-Domain Name | Domain Scope Definition | Assigned Category IDs (Exact 1:N Partition) | Category Count |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :---: |
| **MD-01** | **Terrestrial Trails & Foot Trekking** | Non-technical foot travel on trails, hills, multi-day paths, and high-altitude non-glaciated trekking corridors. | `CAT-01`, `CAT-02`, `CAT-03`, `CAT-04` | 4 |
| **MD-02** | **Technical Rock, Via Ferrata & Big Wall** | Unroped scrambling, protected iron-ways, multi-pitch technical rock, and vertical big-wall aid/free ascents. | `CAT-05`, `CAT-06`, `CAT-07`, `CAT-08` | 4 |
| **MD-03** | **Alpine & High-Altitude Mountaineering** | Glaciated trekking peaks, classic alpine mixed peaks, 6,500m–7,999m expedition peaks, and the 14 Eight-Thousanders (8,000m+). | `CAT-09`, `CAT-10`, `CAT-11`, `CAT-12` | 4 |

| **MD-05** | **Endurance, Overland, Remote & Subterranean** | Ultra-trail mountain endurance, high-pass bikepacking, remote wilderness/packrafting, arid desert crossings, polar ski expeditions, technical canyoneering, and expedition speleology/caving. | `CAT-16`, `CAT-17`, `CAT-18`, `CAT-19`, `CAT-20`, `CAT-21`, `CAT-22` | 7 |
