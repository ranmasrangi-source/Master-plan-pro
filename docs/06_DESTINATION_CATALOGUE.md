## 1. Mandatory Data-Hygiene & Safety Schema (Binding Amendment A6 & CHK-01.14–01.18)
Every single destination record (`DEST-001` through `DEST-100`) enforces the following data-hygiene and safety metadata attributes:

1. **Core Ontological Identity (A7):** `destinationId` (`DEST-001..100`), `entityType` (`MOUNTAIN_MASSIF | ALPINE_PEAK | TREKKING_PEAK | VOLCANO | CORRIDOR_TRAIL | CANYON_SYSTEM | POLAR_PLATEAU`), `regionId` (`REG-*`), `primaryRouteIds[]` (`RTE-*`).
2. **Bilingual Nomenclature (`CHK-01.15`):** `nameEn` (Official English Name), `nameNe` (Official Nepali Devanagari Name `नेपाली` — mandatory for all Himalayan destinations and transliterated across all 100), `indigenousOrAltNames[]`.
3. **Data-Hygiene Columns (Binding Amendment A6):**
   - `source_url`: Direct authoritative reference URL (e.g., `https://nepalhimalpeakprofile.org`, `https://www.himalayandatabase.com`, `https://www.usgs.gov`, `https://www.swisstopo.admin.ch`).
   - `source_type`: Enum (`GOVT_SURVEY_MINISTRY | NATIONAL_PARK_AUTHORITY | OFFICIAL_ALPINE_FEDERATION | ARCHIVAL_DATABASE`).
   - `last_verified_date`: ISO-8601 date (`YYYY-MM-DD`, initialized to `2026-11-12` baseline audit date).
   - `confidence`: Enum (`HIGH_AUTHORITATIVE | MEDIUM_SECONDARY | LOW_PENDING_FIELD_CHECK`).
   - `unverified_fields`: Explicit array of attributes requiring Phase 09 verification (e.g., `["seasonal_bridge_status", "2026_local_municipality_fee", "heli_landing_pad_coords"]`).
4. **Geopolitical Sensitivity Flag (`CHK-01.14`):** `geopoliticalFlag` (`NONE | TRANSBOUNDARY_DUAL_JURISDICTION | RESTRICTED_BORDER_PERMIT | DISPUTED_OR_MILITARY_NOC_ZONE`).
5. **Emergency Contact & Rescue Protocol (`CHK-01.17`):** `sarAgency`, `emergencyContact`, `traumaHospital`, `heliCeilingMeters`, `nonRescueDisclaimer: true`.
6. **Seasonal Risk Rating (`CHK-01.18`):** `seasonalRiskSummary` (`LOW` optimal months, `MEDIUM` shoulder months, `HIGH` monsoon/winter months, `EXTREME_CLOSURE` closed months).
